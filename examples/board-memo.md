# Editing a board memo

Draft:

> The gym opened an hour earlier on Saturdays in October. Our staff logged
> 18 visits before 9 a.m. We should keep opening at 8 a.m. on Saturdays through
> December if we can cover the shift without overtime. We didn't count visits
> after 9 a.m., so we don't know whether the earlier hour brought in more people.

**What I think you're saying.** Keep the earlier Saturday opening through
December if staff can cover it without overtime.

**Where I lose the thread.** "Our staff logged 18 visits before 9 a.m." arrives
before I know what you want the board to decide. On first read, I also can't
tell if you're using the count to claim overall visits went up.

**What I'd change.** Lead with the request and put the limit beside the count.

> We should keep opening the gym at 8 a.m. on Saturdays through December if
> we can cover the shift without overtime.
>
> In October we opened an hour earlier on Saturdays and logged 18 visits before
> 9 a.m. We didn't count visits after 9 a.m., so we don't know whether the
> earlier hour brought in more people.

- Before: "We should keep opening at 8 a.m. on Saturdays through December if
  we can cover the shift without overtime."
- After: "We should keep opening the gym at 8 a.m. on Saturdays through December
  if we can cover the shift without overtime."
- Why: Move the request to the first line and name the gym so the request can
  stand on its own.
