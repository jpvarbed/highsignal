# highsignal deep review

A rigorous review, not a spot-check. Four levels, top down, each a full pass. Nothing ships
until it scores 0 / Clean on the avoid-ai-writing detector and passes every level below.

## The four levels

**1. Whole-piece arc.** State the spine of the piece in one sentence. The first sentence earns
the read. The last one lands. Every paragraph moves the spine forward. If you cannot state the
arc in one sentence, stop and grill the author (see the grill gate) rather than guess.

**2. Paragraph arc.** Each paragraph has one job and a shape: it opens, develops, closes. Delete
any paragraph that survives deletion without loss. Cut any paragraph that restates the one before it.

**3. Sentence prose.** Each sentence is the tightest form of its point. For every sentence
carrying fat, offer the shorter version. Vary sentence length so the rhythm is not a flat row.
Apply the read-aloud test: would a sharp person say this to another person's face?

**4. Sentence AI-tells.** Scan every sentence against the highsignal tell list and the
avoid-ai-writing detector. Zero throat-clears. Zero label-colons. Weird punctuation out.

## Hard rules

- **Remove every throat-clear and label-colon.** "One thing that helps:", "My hardest problem:",
  "Here's the thing." Write the plain sentence.
- **Weird punctuation out.** Em dashes at most ~1 per 100 words, ideally none. No semicolon
  stacking, no ellipsis-as-drama. Target 0 / Clean on the avoid-ai-writing detector.
- **Never add filler.** Cutting is the default. Adding a word needs a reason. A sentence you can
  delete with nothing lost is filler.
- **Always offer the more concise wording.** For any sentence kept as-is, show the tighter version
  so the author chooses.

## The grill gate

If level 1 or 2 fails and the fix is not obvious from the text, do not invent a narrative to paper
over it. Invoke the grilling skill and interview the author: what is the one point, who is it for,
what should the reader do next. Restructure from their answers. A manufactured arc is worse than an
honest question.

## Adversarial fan-out (for pieces that matter)

For product-critical writing, do not review it once with one set of eyes. Fan out independent
reviewers, one lens each, then synthesize:

- **arc.** Does the whole piece and each paragraph carry one spine?
- **concision.** Which sentences carry fat, and what is the tighter form?
- **tells.** Throat-clears, label-colons, filler, em-dash overuse, every highsignal tell.
- **hook.** Does the opener earn the read without manufactured drama?
- **read-aloud.** Would a sharp person say each line to someone's face?

Each reviewer returns findings scored against this rubric. A synthesis pass dedupes and ranks.
Verify each finding against the text before applying it. This is the review-council pattern aimed
at prose instead of code.

## Done

- Arc stateable in one sentence, holds across every paragraph.
- No deletable paragraph, no deletable sentence.
- 0 / Clean on the avoid-ai-writing detector.
- Every kept sentence is its tightest form, or the author chose to keep the longer one.
